1.0      DEVICE DESCRIPTION
         The TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter (Guide) provides a conduit to access the right
         atrium. The distal curve is optimal for achieving height above tricuspid valve plane. The
         distal shaft is steered by the use of a handle and knobs (S/L and +/-) to position the
         implant on the tricuspid valve. The Guide tip is radiopaque. The Guide is fitted with a
         valve to provide hemostasis during introduction and / or exchange of ancillary devices
         (e.g., catheters) that have a maximum diameter of 5.2 mm (.204”). A flush port is
         provided on the valve housing for aspiration, fluid infusion, and blood sampling via a
         three way stopcock attachment. The Guide includes a Dilator that is tapered at the distal
         tip and includes an echogenic feature for visualization under imaging. The Dilator has an
         internal lumen designed to accept ancillary devices that have a maximum diameter of
         0.9 mm (0.035”) (e.g., needles or guidewires). The distal 65 cm of the Steerable Guide
         Catheter with the exception of the distal soft tip is coated with hydrophilic coating to
         provide a lubricated surface when hydrated with sterile saline for ease of insertion.

2.0      INDICATION FOR USE
         The TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter is used for introducing various cardiovascular
         catheters into the right side of the heart.

3.0      CONTRAINDICATIONS
         •     Intolerance, including allergy or untreatable hypersensitivity, to procedural
               anticoagulation.
         •     Active endocarditis or other active infection of the tricuspid valve.

4.0   WARNINGS
      •     Read all instructions carefully. Failure to follow these instructions, warnings and
            precautions may lead to device damage, user injury or patient injury including:
               o Failure to deliver the TriClip™ G5 Implant to the intended site
               o Difficulty or failure to retrieve TriClip™ G5 System components
      •     Use universal precautions for biohazards and sharps while handling the TriClip™
            G5 Steerable Guide Catheter to avoid user injury.
      •     Failure to prepare the device as stated in these instructions and failure to handle
            the device with care might result in damage to the device coating, which may lead
            to additional intervention or serious adverse events.

5.0   PRECAUTIONS
      •     Use the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter with sterile techniques using
            standard imaging techniques (e.g., fluoroscopy and transesophageal
            echocardiography).
      •     Echocardiographic images should be carefully assessed to ensure they are of
            adequate quality to allow successful implantation of the TriClip™ G5 Implant using
            the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter.
      •     The TriClip™ G5 Procedure using the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter should
            be considered with caution in patients with rheumatic disease who have significant
            leaflet thickening and small annular dimensions considering the risk of iatrogenic
            tricuspid stenosis.
      •     Patients with pre-existing cardiac leads should be assessed to ensure placement of
            the TriClip™ Implant is possible using the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter.

6.0   SPECIAL FACILITIES
      Facilities using the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter need to maintain a plan for
      treating the patient if cardiovascular surgical intervention is required.

7.0   HOW SUPPLIED
      7.1     Contents:
              One (1) TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter, one (1) Dilator, and one (1)
              Silicone Pad.
      7.2     Sterile:
              The TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter is sterilized with ethylene oxide gas
              and is provided in a tray with lid, in a sealed pouch. Parts of the device that are in
              either direct or indirect contact with circulating blood are non-pyrogenic.
8.0   STORAGE
      Handle with care. Store in original packaging. Keep dry. Keep away from sunlight.

10.0   PATIENT PREPARATION
       WARNING: When placing a TriClip™ G5 Implant using the TriClip™ G5
                Steerable Guide Catheter, refer to the TriClip™ G5 System
                Instructions For Use.
       WARNING: Patient selection should be performed by a multi-disciplinary heart
                team. Refer to the TriClip™ G5 System Instructions For Use for
                patient selection and potential adverse events information.
       Prepare the patient per institution’s standard practice for endovascular and transcatheter
       procedures. Prepare the patient for invasive hemodynamic monitoring.

11.0   DEVICE PREPARATION BEFORE USE
       The preparation is most easily accomplished with the aid of an assistant.

       Inspect all product prior to use; do not use if the device or packaging is damaged. Note
       the product “Use By” date specified on the package; do not use if the “Use By” date has
       passed. These devices are intended for single-use only; do not reuse.
       WARNING:       DO NOT use the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide after the “Use
                      By” date stated on the package label, and never reuse or re-
                      sterilize the Steerable Guide Catheter. Use of expired, reused,
                      or re-sterilized devices may result in infection, malfunction of
                      the device and other serious injury or death.
       WARNING:       Always inspect the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter and its
                      packaging to verify no damage has occurred as a result of
                      shipping and handling and that the sterile barrier has not been
                      compromised. DO NOT use the device if damage is detected. Use
                      of product with a compromised sterile barrier may result in
                      infection. Use of damaged product may result in patient injury.

12.0   TRICLIP™ G5 STEERABLE GUIDE CATHETER PREPARATION
       WARNING:       All lumens contain air when shipped. Use proper de-airing
                      techniques before and during use to minimize the risk of air
                      embolism.
       12.1   Remove the Guide from sterile packaging and place in the sterile field.
       12.2   Carefully remove the white Guide tip shape retainer and transparent protective
              tubing from the Guide tip.

       12.3  Inspect the Guide and Dilator to verify they are undamaged.
             WARNING: DO NOT use if damage is detected. Use of damaged product
                            may result in air embolism, vascular and / or cardiac injury.
       12.4 Remove the sterile package containing the Silicone Pad from the Guide tray.
       12.5 Fill a basin with heparinized saline.
       12.6 Flush and de-air the Guide and Dilator with heparinized saline:
             12.6.1 Connect 3-way stopcocks to the Guide and Dilator ﬂush ports, de-air the
                       Dilator, then close the stopcock and the Rotating Hemostatic Valve.
             12.6.2 Hydrate 5-10 cm of the distal end of the Dilator with heparinized saline
                       and insert the Dilator approximately 10 cm into Guide then remove.
             12.6.3 Connect high pressure tubing and a 50-60 cc syringe ﬁlled with
                       heparinized saline to the Guide ﬂush port.
             12.6.4 De-air the Guide.
                       12.6.4.1       With the tip raised, displace all air from the Guide while
                                      tapping along the length of the catheter shaft.
                       12.6.4.2       Cover the Guide tip with finger once heparinized saline
                                      exits the Guide.
                       12.6.4.3       Close the Guide stopcock.
       12.7 Submerge the Guide tip in the basin of heparinized saline.
       12.8 While the Guide tip is submerged in the basin of heparinized saline, remove
             finger from Guide tip and check the Guide valve for leaks by raising the handle to
             a vertical position for a minimum of 30 seconds.
       12.9 Hydrate 5-10 cm of the distal end of the Dilator with heparinized saline.
       12.10 Cover the Guide tip with finger and insert the Dilator into the Guide while Guide
             tip remains submerged in the basin of heparinized saline.
             12.10.1 While advancing the Dilator, continually watch for air in the Guide
                       Hemostasis Valve housing. If needed, remove finger from Guide tip
                       and aspirate while assuring the Guide tip is submerged.
             12.10.2 Remove finger from tip of Guide when the Dilator tip approaches the
                       Guide tip.
             12.10.3 Position the Dilator to create a smooth transition.

       WARNING:       Conﬁrm a smooth transition between the Dilator and the tip of the
                      Guide to minimize the risk of vascular and / or cardiovascular
                      injury.

13.0   ACCESS TO THE TRICUSPID VALVE
       NOTE:         This is a suggested sequence for the procedure. Variations may be
                     used based upon patient anatomy.
       13.1    Access the RA to accommodate the Guide tip using transvenous techniques and
               equipment.
       13.2    Heparinize the patient.
               WARNING:       Failure to administer heparin may result in thrombus
                              formation.

14.0   TRICLIP™ G5 STEERABLE GUIDE CATHETER INSERTION
       CAUTION:      Always use pressure monitoring, echocardiography and
                     ﬂuoroscopy for guidance and observation during use of the
                     TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter.
       WARNING:      Always use a careful, deliberate, and iterative approach to
                     positioning the TriClip™ G5 Steerable Guide Catheter. It is
                     recommended to make multiple small adjustments rather than
                     single large adjustments. Large adjustments may result in vascular
                     and / or cardiac injury.

       14.1   Rotate the +/- Knob in the “-” direction until the Guide curve is
              substantially straightened.
       14.2   Wet the surface of the Guide shaft with sterile saline.
       14.3   Insert the Guide-Dilator assembly over the stationary guidewire into the
              femoral vein.
              WARNING:       DO NOT use excessive force to advance or manipulate the
                             Guide-Dilator assembly. If resistance is encountered, use
                             echocardiography and / or ﬂuoroscopy to assess before
                             proceeding. Use of excessive force may result in
                             arrhythmias, vascular and / or cardiac injury.
       14.4   Advance the Guide-Dilator assembly to the RA maintaining the Guide in a
              straightened position.
       14.5   Adjust Guide torque to position the tip away from adjacent tissues.
       14.6   Place the Silicone Pad on the sterile drape over the Lift. Place the Stabilizer onto
              the Silicone Pad, such that the Stabilizer Guide Dock is underneath the Guide
              Attach.
       14.7   Rotate the Guide Attach independently from the Guide as necessary to align the
              Guide Attach pins with the Stabilizer Guide Dock.
       14.8   Insert the Guide Attach into the Stabilizer Guide Dock.
              CAUTION:       Ensure that the Stabilizer is secure on the lift to prevent any
                             undesirable movement.
       14.9   Retract the Dilator approximately 5 cm into the Guide, leaving the guide wire in
              the superior vena cava.
       14.10 Retract the guidewire into the tip of the Dilator. Remove the Dilator and
             guidewire while gently aspirating the Guide (starting when the Dilator is
             approximately halfway retracted into the Guide, approximately 40 cm) using a
             50–60 cc syringe. Cover Guide Hemostasis Valve with finger upon Dilator
             removal.

15.0   GUIDE REMOVAL AFTER DEVICE USE
       15.1     Straighten the Guide with the +/- Knob and turn the S/L Knob to neutral.
       15.2     Carefully retract the Guide tip into the RA / Inferior Vena Cava junction. The
                Guide may be straightened further with the +/- Knob if desired.
       15.3     Retract the Guide towards the femoral vein then remove the Guide while
                providing hemostasis.

16.0   DISPOSAL
       After use, these devices and packaging should be appropriately classified for disposal,
       e.g., biohazard, sharps, non-hazardous waste etc., and carefully disposed of in
       compliance with facility procedures and applicable laws and regulations.
