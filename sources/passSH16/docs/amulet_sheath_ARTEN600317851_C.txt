Device Description
The Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath (STRBL-14F-075) is designed to provide a pathway through which a device may be delivered. The
sheath is provided in one size (14Fr), with a working length of 75 cm and a bidirectional distal tip to provide positioning in the cardiac
anatomy. The handle is equipped with a deflection knob to deflect the tip clockwise 120° and counterclockwise 0°. The body of the sheath
is radiopaque for visibility under fluoroscopy and has a marker band located in the distal tip. The dilator eases penetration of tissue. The
sheath and dilator utilize a dual curve in two dimensions, resulting in a three-dimensional geometry. The 14Fr flush adapter facilitates the
attachment of additional components.

Indication for Use
The Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath is indicated to facilitate the delivery of the Amplatzer™ Amulet™ Left Atrial Appendage Occluder.

Contraindications
The Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath is contraindicated for patients:

                                                                      2
   • with the presence of thrombus that is in the pathway of the steerable sheath, including within the left atrial appendage
   • with active infections including endocarditis
The Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath is contraindicated for use with a power injection system.

Warnings
  • This device was sterilized with ethylene oxide and is for single use only. Do not reuse or resterilize this device. Attempts to resterilize
    this device can cause a malfunction, insufficient sterilization, or harm to the patient.
  • Do not use this device if the sterile package is open or damaged. Inspect all components before use. Do not use if the package or items
    appear to be damaged or defective.
  • This device should be used only by physicians who are trained in standard transcatheter techniques. The physician should determine
    which patients are candidates for procedures that use this device.
  • Slowly remove the dilator and sheath from the patient to prevent an ingress of air.
  • As this medical device uses an alternative small-bore connector (luer) design different from those of the ISO 80369 series, there is a
    possibility that a misconnection can occur between this medical device and a medical device using a different alternative small-bore
    connector, which can result in a hazardous situation causing harm to the patient. Special measures need be taken by the user to
    mitigate these reasonable, foreseeable risks. See call-out 3 in Figure 1 for an illustration of the small-bore connector (luer).
  • Failure to cap the sheath hub once the dilator has been removed may result in excessive bleeding or air ingress.
  • When removing the delivery sheath from the patient, failure to return the delivery sheath to a neutral position may result in vascular
    or cardiac injury.
  • Do not deflect the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath to its maximum deflection more than 10 times during the procedure.

Precautions
   • DO NOT use the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath after the Use-by date stated on the package label.
   • The physician should exercise clinical judgment in situations that involve the use of anticoagulants or antiplatelet drugs before, during,
     or after the use of the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath.
   • When placing an Amplatzer™ Amulet™ Left Atrial Appendage Occluder using the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath, refer also to the
     instructions for use provided with the Amplatzer™ Amulet™ Left Atrial Appendage Occluder.
   • Do not over-tighten the connection between the dilator rotating luer and delivery sheath hub.
   • Use caution when advancing the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath and dilator to avoid damaging tissue and vessels or interfering
     with previously implanted medical devices. If resistance is felt, determine the cause before proceeding. Continuing to advance, retract,
     or manipulate the catheter under this circumstance has the potential to result in patient injury or delivery sheath damage.

Limitations
Certain conditions may require special consideration when using this product. These may be, but are not limited to calcified or thickened
interatrial septum, or cases with previously implanted medical devices (for example, a septal occluder or transvenous pacing leads)

Device Compatibility
This device is compatible with the Amplatzer™ Amulet™ loader. A standard hypodermic syringe may be attached to the sheath hub by first
attaching the 14Fr flush adapter, and then connecting the syringe to the flush adapter.

Performance Characteristics
The Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath consists of the following:

                                                                       3
   • Steerable Delivery Sheath
   • Dilator
   • 14Fr flush adapter
With a working length of 75 cm and an inner diameter that is 14Fr, the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath provides flexible catheter
positioning in the cardiac anatomy. The delivery sheath handle is equipped with a handle deflection knob to deflect the tip bidirectionally.
The pre-set bidirectional curve aligns with typical left atrial appendage anatomy (see Figure 1 and Figure 2). The delivery sheath includes a
radiopaque tip marker to improve fluoroscopic visualization.

Storage and Handling Information
   • There are no special storage requirements beyond what is typical for medical devices.
   • The instructions for use is recyclable. Dispose of all packaging materials appropriately.

Directions for Use
Materials recommended for use with the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath:
  • 0.035-inch guidewire
  • 19Fr or larger introducer sheath
  • 30cc-60cc luer-lock syringe
  • 5Fr - 6Fr marker pigtail catheter, 110 cm length minimum
  • Hemostasis valve
Procedure
       CAUTION: When placing an Amplatzer™ Amulet™ Left Atrial Appendage Occluder using the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath,
       refer also to the instructions for use provided with the device.
    1. Prepare the Amulet™ Steerable Delivery Sheath for use:
        - Inspect the sterile pouch and verify that it is unopened and undamaged. Do not use the components if the sterile barrier has
          been compromised.
        - Gently open the sterile pouch and inspect the contents for damage. Do not use damaged or kinked components.
        - Ensure the delivery sheath curve can be easily deflected. Do not use the delivery sheath if there is difficulty in deflecting the distal
          section to achieve or hold the desired curve.
        - Flush the components with sterile saline.
        NOTE: Connect the 14Fr flush adapter to the sheath hub before connecting a syringe to flush.
       - Wipe the delivery sheath and dilator with sterile gauze dampened with sterile saline.
    2. Insert the dilator into the delivery sheath and turn the rotating luer on the dilator clockwise to lock the components together.
        NOTE: You may encounter resistance as the dilator reaches the distal end of the delivery sheath because the tip of the sheath is
        tapered.
        CAUTION: Do not over-tighten the connection.
    3. Access the left atrium using transvenous and transseptal techniques and equipment.
        NOTE: After transseptal puncture is performed, maintain the recommended activated clotting time (ACT) of 250 to 350 seconds
        until the procedure is complete.
    4. Place a 0.035-inch guidewire with the distal end in the left upper pulmonary vein.
    5. Advance the delivery sheath and dilator over the guidewire until the delivery sheath is positioned according to the
       Amplatzer™ Amulet™ Left Atrial Appendage Occluder instructions.
        CAUTION: If resistance is encountered, use available imaging modalities to determine the cause before proceeding. Use of
        excessive force may result in patient injury or delivery sheath damage. The delivery sheath may be retracted slightly and
        straightened prior to being re-advanced.
    6. Turn the rotating luer on the dilator counter-clockwise to unlock the components. Slowly remove the dilator and guidewire from
       the delivery sheath.
        WARNING: Remove the dilator and guidewire slowly to prevent air ingress.
    7. To prevent blood loss and avoid air ingress, use a hemostasis valve or otherwise ensure that the sheath hub is capped until ready to
       connect the device loader.
        WARNING: Failure to cap the sheath hub may result in excessive bleeding or air embolism.
        WARNING: The sheath hub is a custom luer fitting and is only compatible with the supplied 14Fr flush adapter, 14Fr sheath
        adapter, and the device’s 14Fr loader. Do not attempt to directly connect any other accessories to the sheath hub.
   8.   Remove the hemostasis valve or cap that was placed on the sheath hub after removal of the dilator. Allow fluid backflow to purge
        air from the delivery sheath by holding the sheath hub below the plane of the patient’s body.
   9.   Immediately connect the loader of the device to the sheath in a fluid-to-fluid fashion, according to the device’s instructions for use.
   10. Advance the device to the tip of the delivery sheath per the device’s instructions for use.


                                                                        4
   11. Adjust the tip of the delivery sheath by turning the handle deflection knob to achieve the desired position in the patient’s anatomy.
        NOTE: Deflection direction is shown in graphics on the handle.
   12. Implant the occluder according to the device’s instructions for use.
   13. When the procedure is complete, return the tip of the delivery sheath to a neutral (or atraumatic) position, and slowly remove the
       delivery sheath.
        WARNING: Remove the delivery sheath slowly to prevent air ingress.
        WARNING: Failure to return the delivery sheath to a neutral position may result in vascular or cardiac injury.

Post-procedure Care
Instruct the patient when to seek medical attention.
